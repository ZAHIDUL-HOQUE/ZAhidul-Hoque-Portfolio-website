Prototypical Mask R-CNN for Vehicle Exterior Damage Detection

University of Hertfordshire • Degree: M.Sc. in Data Science and Analytics with Advanced Research • Supervisor: Dr. Joseph Reddington • Module: 7COM1039-0901-2025 (Advanced Computer Science Masters Project)

Architecture Prototypical Mask R-CNN
Backbone ResNet-50-FPN (MS-COCO)
Benchmark (CarDD) 4,000 Images (810 Val)
mAP (0.50:0.95) 0.319 (Peak ~32.2%)
AP@50 • F1-Score 0.630 • 0.515
Average Recall (AR) 0.436 (MaxDets=100)

📑 Abstract & Research Questions

Automated vehicle damage assessment promises to replace subjective, slow manual insurance surveys with consistent visual audit trails. However, real-world vehicle damage datasets exhibit severe long-tailed class imbalance (an 11.4 : 1 ratio in CarDD) and high visual overlap between categories such as scratches and structural cracks. Standard two-stage detectors relying on parametric linear classification heads (Score = Wx + b) suffer from majority-class gradient dominance and fail to separate visually similar or underrepresented defects.

Heavily inspired by Prototypical Networks (Snell et al., 2017) and Meta R-CNN predictor head remodelling (Yan et al., 2019), this study develops a hybrid Prototypical Mask R-CNN that replaces the fully connected linear classifier with a Squared Euclidean Prototypical Box Predictor, classifying Regions of Interest (RoIs) by minimising distance to learnable class centroids on a unit hypersphere rather than learning separating hyperplanes.

🎯
Core Research Questions Investigated:
  1. RQ1: Can a metric learning approach like Prototypical Networks be integrated into Mask R-CNN to generalize effectively across imbalanced and novel vehicle damage classes?
  2. RQ2: How does the integration of a Squared Euclidean Prototypical Head within a Mask R-CNN framework influence the geometric arrangement of the 1024-D latent feature space?
  3. RQ3: What is the measured performance in F1-Score (0.515) and Average Recall (AR = 0.436) achieved on minority classes, and does the architecture mitigate the bias inherent in long-tail distributions?

🔬 Methodology, EDA & Architectural Contributions

1. Predictor Head Remodelling

Replaced the standard Mask R-CNN linear classification layer with a custom PrototypicalBoxPredictor containing static, Xavier-initialized learnable class prototypes (num_classes × 1024), operating in parallel with a linear bounding-box coordinate regressor and a decoupled 28 × 28 Fully Convolutional Network (FCN) binary mask branch after bilinear RoIAlign.

2. Hyperspherical Latent Geometry

Enforced strict L2 normalisation on both 1024-D RoI embeddings (x̂ = x / ‖x‖2) and class prototypes (P̂k = Pk / ‖Pk‖2), computing logits via temperature-scaled Negative Squared Euclidean Distance:

Sk = −α · ‖x̂ − P̂k‖22   (α = 20.0)
d2(pi, pj) = ‖pi − pj‖22 = 2 − 2cos(θ)

On the unit hypersphere (‖p‖2 = 1), a squared distance of 2.00 proves 90° orthogonal separation between damage classes.

3. CarDD Statistical & Geometric EDA

Analyzed all 4,000 high-resolution CarDD images (>9,000 pixel-level instances across 2,817 train / 810 val / 373 test): quantified the 11.4 : 1 head-to-tail imbalance, applied Log2 Aspect Ratio transformation (log2(W/H)) to contrast square dents (0) against elongated scratches (−3 to +3), evaluated √Area scale variance, and mapped 2D spatial Photographer Centre Bias.

4. Augmentation & Spatial Consistency

Mitigated specular paint reflections and uncontrolled lighting via stochastic Color Jitter (Brightness ±0.2, Contrast ±0.2, Saturation ±0.2) and random horizontal flipping (p = 0.5) with synchronous bounding-box coordinate inversion and binary mask mirroring (torchvision.transforms.functional.hflip).

5. Mixed-Precision Training Dynamics

Trained for 30 epochs on an NVIDIA RTX 3060 (12GB VRAM, Batch Size = 4, NMS = 0.25) using torch.cuda.amp.GradScaler (FP16/FP32), SGD (lr = 0.001, momentum = 0.9, weight_decay = 0.0005), StepLR (decay ×0.1 every 5 epochs), and gradient clipping (max_norm = 5.0). Training loss dropped from 0.90 → 0.65 (Epochs 1–5), formed an elbow at Epoch 6, and plateaued smoothly at 0.59 from Epoch 11.

6. Scale Bottleneck & Future VLA Roadmap

Identified a critical resolution bottleneck between large defects (APLarge = 0.320) and microscopic defects under 322 px (APSmall = 0.074). Proposed projecting the 1024-D prototypical damage embeddings into Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs) and Vision-Language-Action (VLA) frameworks (Hu et al., 2025) for automated severity reasoning.
